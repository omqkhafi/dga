## Usage

```tsx title="page.tsx"
import { Carousel } from "@/components/dga/carousel";

export function Featured() {
  return (
    <Carousel aria-label="Featured services" controls="dots">
      <Slide title="Renew your licence online" />
      <Slide title="Track your request" />
      <Slide title="Pay securely" />
    </Carousel>
  );
}
```

Each child is one slide. Use a carousel to show a few equal items in the space of one. Do not hide content people need to compare or must read. Name it with `aria-label` and translate `previousLabel`, `nextLabel`, `pauseLabel`, `playLabel`, `slideLabel`, `dotLabel` and `dotsLabel` on Arabic pages.

The first slide is on the right, previous is on the right and its arrow points right, and a swipe to the right shows the next slide.

## Anatomy

- **Slides**: one visible at a time, moved by the track. In a right-to-left page the first slide is on the right and the track moves the other way.
- **Controls** (`controls`): `arrows` (previous and next beside the slides), `dots` (arrows and dots) or `dots-only`.
- **Arrows**: 32, 40 or 48px (`size`), `primary` (brand fill, on-colour icon) or `subtle` (`arrowStyle`). They are disabled at the ends unless `loop`.
- **Dots**: 8, 12 or 16px (`dotSize`) inside a 24px target. The chosen dot is filled and the others are rings.
- **Pause button**: shown when `autoPlay` is on.

### Autoplay

`autoPlay` is a time in milliseconds. It stops while the pointer or focus is inside, can be paused, and never starts when the person prefers reduced motion.

## Accessibility

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd>Tab</kbd> | Moves through the pause button, previous, next and the dots. Slides that are not visible are skipped. |
| <kbd>Left</kbd> / <kbd>Right</kbd> | Previous or next slide while focus is inside. They swap on Arabic pages. |
| <kbd>Home</kbd> / <kbd>End</kbd> | First or last slide. |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> / <kbd>Space</kbd> | Presses the focused button. |

- A `section` with `aria-roledescription="carousel"`. Each slide is a group named "1 of 4" (`slideLabel`); slides that are not visible are `aria-hidden` and `inert`.
- Moving content can be paused (WCAG 2.2.2). While it plays the live region is off, so a screen reader is not interrupted; otherwise it is polite.
- On touch screens a horizontal swipe moves one slide; vertical scrolling still works.
- Arrows and dots are 3:1, and so is the focus ring, in light and dark.

## Deviations from DGA

- Figma has no behaviour. Autoplay is off by default, with a pause button, and respects reduced motion.
- Arrows are previous and next, not left and right, and mirror in RTL.
- The dots are drawn at 8 to 16px but are 24px targets, and the chosen one is filled while the others are rings.
